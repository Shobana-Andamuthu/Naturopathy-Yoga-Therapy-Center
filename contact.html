Preparing for Your First Visit

A few simple considerations to ensure your time with us is completely restful, unhurried, and comfortable from the moment you arrive.

01

Arrive 15 Mins Early

Allow yourself time to transition from the commute, enjoy a warm herbal infusion in our quiet lounge, and settle in.

02

Comfortable Attire

For yoga therapy and lifestyle evaluations, wear loose-fitting, breathable natural fabrics that permit unhindered movement.

03

Health History

Bring any previous laboratory findings or medication lists so your naturopathic doctor has complete context.

04

Itemized Receipts

Downloadable invoices and health expense statements are automatically generated in your patient dashboard after each visit.
