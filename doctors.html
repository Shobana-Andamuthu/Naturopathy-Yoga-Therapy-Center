Care Methodology

How We Listen, Understand, and Guide Your Healing

At Sattva, clinical consultations are never rushed. We recognize that physical tension, irregular sleep, or low energy are often interconnected signs of daily lifestyle imbalance rather than isolated occurrences.

01

Unhurried Active Listening

Your practitioner listens carefully to your personal history, emotional stress triggers, and wellness aspirations without judgment.

02

Constitutional Understanding

We evaluate your body’s unique physical tendencies, posture, and vital capacity to understand what natural therapies will best serve you.

03

Empowered Co-Creation

Together, we agree on a practical regimen of in-center therapies and achievable home self-care habits that fit seamlessly into your life.
